Apartment Wolfgangsee in Gosau

Self check-in apartment of 105 m² on two floors for 4 to 8 guests

The apartment

Apartment Wolfgangsee is our large apartment: 105 m² on two floors for families and groups of friends. There's room for everyone at the long table with its corner bench, and the kitchen is equipped for big gatherings.

Two bedrooms with double beds, a high-quality 180 × 200 cm sofa bed and a pull-out sofa in the living room sleep up to eight.

The apartment is on two floors connected by a staircase.

Namesake and day trip

Wolfgangsee

50 km · 50 min from the house

At the Wolfgangsee, St. Wolfgang with the famous White Horse Inn and the rack railway up the Schafberg await. A day trip for the whole group.

Good to know

  • Check-in from 3:30 pm to 10 pm, contactless with a code. Check-out by 10 am
  • You receive your door code by email as soon as the guest registration form is complete for everyone travelling with you, at the latest 48 hours before arrival
  • 30% deposit by bank transfer within 7 days of booking
  • Security deposit of € 300 pre-authorised on your credit card, released after departure
  • Final cleaning is included in the price
  • Tourist tax € 4.00 per person per night, children up to 15 exempt
  • Cancellation under AGBH 2006: free up to 3 months before, then 40%, from 1 month before 70%, in the last week 90%
  • Pets are not allowed in the apartments, but they are at the Schmiedsipplhof farm
  • Bus 541/542, Gosau Sommerhof stop, 20 m from the house
