Privacy policy

Protecting your data matters to us. We process it solely on the basis of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Austrian Data Protection Act (DSG) and the Telecommunications Act (TKG 2021). Here you can find out which data we process on this website and why. The German version of this policy is legally binding.

1. Controller

Schmez Holding GmbH
Austraße 22, 4825 Gosau, Austria
Phone +43 664 11 44 926
Email: info@apartments-salzkammergut.at

2. Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by our provider Web Experts Austria in Austria. Each time a page is accessed, the server automatically stores technical data: IP address, date and time, the page requested, browser and operating system, and the previously visited page. We need this data to run the website securely and reliably (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). It is not combined with other data and is deleted after 30 days at the latest, unless a security incident needs to be investigated.

3. Fonts

All fonts are stored on our own server. No connection to Google or other font providers is made when you visit the website.

4. Contact by email or phone

If you contact us by email or phone, we process your details (name, contact details, travel dates, request) to answer your enquiry and prepare a booking (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We do not pass this data on without your consent. Enquiries that do not lead to a booking are deleted after 12 months at the latest.

5. Online booking

For bookings, the “Book now” and “Check availability” buttons take you to an external booking system, where you enter your details. The system is run by its operator on our behalf as a processor. We receive the data we need for your booking (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We keep booking and invoice data for seven years as required by the Austrian Federal Fiscal Code (§ 132 BAO). For the statutory guest registration under the Austrian Registration Act and the Gosau tourist tax, we also process the guest data required by law (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

6. Cookies and local storage

Without your consent we do not set any cookies for analytics or advertising. We only store in your browser whether or not you agreed to statistics, so we don't ask you again on every visit. This storage is technically necessary (§ 165(3) TKG 2021). You can change your choice at any time via “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page.

7. Google Analytics (only with your consent)

If you agree via “Allow statistics”, we use Google Analytics 4 by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Google Analytics helps us understand how many guests visit our website and which pages they view. For this, Google sets cookies and processes shortened IP addresses, device and browser data and your usage behaviour on this website. Data may also be transferred to Google LLC in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, which ensures an adequate level of protection (Art. 45 GDPR). The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 165(3) TKG 2021). The data is deleted after 14 months. You can withdraw your consent at any time via “Cookie settings”, with effect from that moment.

8. Links to Facebook and Instagram

Our website links to our pages on Facebook and Instagram. These are simple links without embedded content or plugins. Only when you click a link do you leave our website, and the privacy policy of Meta Platforms Ireland Limited then applies.

9. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). You can withdraw any consent you have given at any time. Just write to us at info@apartments-salzkammergut.at.

If you believe we are not processing your data correctly, you can lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority: Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, dsb@dsb.gv.at, www.dsb.gv.at.

10. Data security

The connection to our website is encrypted (https). Please do not send us payment details by email.
