Apartment Gosausee in Gosau

Self check-in apartment of 32 m² with balcony for 2 guests

The apartment

Apartment Gosausee is our apartment for two. In 32 m² you have a bright living and sleeping area with a double bed and sofa, a kitchen with a wooden island and a bathroom with shower.

From the balcony you look out over the ski slope and the mountains around Gosau. In winter you walk to the Hornspitz lift, in summer your hikes start right at the door.

Namesake and day trip

Gosausee

7 km · 10 min from the house

The Vorderer Gosausee lies at the end of the valley. On calm days the Dachstein glacier is reflected in the water. The loop trail takes about an hour.
