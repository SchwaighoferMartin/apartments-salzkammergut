Apartment Traunsee in Gosau

Self check-in apartment of 50 m² on two levels for 2 to 4 guests

The apartment

Apartment Traunsee is spread over two levels. Downstairs are the kitchen, dining area, living room, bathroom and WC. Stairs lead up to the gallery with the bedroom under the wooden ceiling.

It's spacious for two, and with the fold-out sofa in the living room four people sleep comfortably.

The gallery is reached by a steep staircase. Apartment Hallstättersee is better suited for small children.

Namesake and day trip

Traunsee

60 km · 55 min from the house

At 191 m, the Traunsee is Austria's deepest lake. In Gmunden you can stroll to Seeschloss Ort and buy pottery straight from the manufactory.
