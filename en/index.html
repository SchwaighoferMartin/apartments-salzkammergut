Location & getting here

Gosauseestraße 57, 4824 Gosau

By car

In Gosau, follow the road towards Gosausee and Hintertal. The apartments are opposite Hotel Sommerhof. The entrance to the underground garage is right at the building, and there are also outdoor spaces in front of the house.

By train and bus

Take the train to Steeg-Gosau, then bus 541 or 542 towards Gosausee to the Gosau Sommerhof stop, 20 m from the house. Timetables at oebb.at and postbus.at.

In winter

The Hornspitz lift in the Dachstein West ski area is just a few minutes' walk away, and the cross-country trail starts behind the house.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know

How does self check-in work?

After booking, you fill in the online guest registration form for everyone travelling with you, as required by Austrian law. As soon as it is 100% complete, we email you your code for the front door, apartment and ski and bike room, at the latest 48 hours before arrival. Check-in from 3:30 pm, check-out by 10:00 am. No key needed.

Can I arrive late in the evening?

Yes. You can check in from 3:30 pm until 10 pm at the latest. Thanks to the code, you don't have to wait for anyone.

What do I do if the code doesn't work?

Call Martin on +43 680 2424007. He'll give you the code for the key safe at the house so you can get in.

How do I pay, is there a deposit?

You pay a 30% deposit by bank transfer within 7 days of booking. As a security deposit we pre-authorise € 300 on your credit card. Nothing is charged as long as nothing is damaged, and we release the amount after you leave.

What does a cancellation cost?

The Austrian General Terms and Conditions for the Hotel Industry (AGBH 2006) apply. Cancellation is free up to 3 months before arrival. From 3 months to 1 month before, 40% is charged; from 1 month to 1 week before, 70%; in the last week and for early departure, 100% of the booking value.

Where do I park?

Each apartment has its own space in the underground garage, which also has charging points for electric cars. There are outdoor spaces as well.

Are pets allowed?

Unfortunately not in the apartments. With a dog, you are very welcome at our Schmiedsipplhof farm: www.almromantik.at

What is included in the price?

Bed linen, towels, Wi-Fi, parking and final cleaning. The only extra is the Gosau tourist tax: € 4.00 per person per night, children up to 15 are exempt.

Do I get the SalzkammergutCard?

Yes. From 3 nights you get the SalzkammergutCard free of charge, with up to 25% off cable cars, caves, boat trips, museums and more. We give you the guest form, and you pick up the card at the Gosau tourist office.

How short-notice can I book?

You can book the apartments even on the day of arrival, as long as they are available.

How do I get to Hallstatt without a car?

Take bus 541 or 542 from the Gosau Sommerhof stop outside the house and change towards Hallstatt. Current connections at oebb.at.
